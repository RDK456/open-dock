# Brag Plan: Open Dock

## What is this app?
Open Dock turns your phone into a PC dock: media keys, hotkeys, app launchers, live PC stats, voice, touchpad, and now a webcam and a desktop pet. Free and open source, Wi-Fi or USB.

## The angle
Last video said "your phone is a PC dock". This one says the dock kept growing: the same phone is also a webcam, a touchpad, a voice remote and the home of a pet. Smooth, tactile motion, each feature shown being used.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark navy background, Open Dock icon, "Your phone is a PC dock." settling in word by word.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Deck: tap Volume up (PC volume bar rises), long-press Mute (second action pops up), swipe to the live stats page (CPU / GPU / RAM / temps).
- Camera: "Use this phone as a webcam for your PC." Waiting → Live, tap 2× zoom, tap Front.
- Pet: a pet on your PC that plays games, wears outfits, reacts to your music.
- Touch + voice: two fingers scroll, pinch zooms; say "hey dock".
- Connect: real screenshot, "Pair once. Switch modes without scanning again."

## Outro / punchline
"Free and open source." + github.com/RDK456/open-dock, landing on the 21.0s strong beat.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: deck on phone → key action: tap / long-press / swipe → result: PC volume rises, stats page appears. Then camera goes live on the PC.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: smooth feature-card film, calm confidence
- Interpretation: slide/fade transitions, short holds on every label, small satisfying taps instead of big effects.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project, current dark theme)
- Background: #05070d (panel #0f1424, line #262e4d)
- Accent: #cfd2ff
- Text: #f2f4ff (muted #9aa3c7)
- Display/body font: system sans-serif (the phone app uses Roboto-like sans)
- Strongest visual element: the real Connect screen (new screenshot); deck, camera, pet recreated in HTML from the app's UI.

Privacy note: the author's phone wallpaper and PC hostname are not shown; those screens are recreated with neutral stand-ins ("My PC").

## Share copy (draft)
Open Dock turns your phone into a PC dock, a webcam, a touchpad, a voice remote and a desktop pet. Free and open source.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM), trimmed to 24s, fade-in 0.4s, fade-out 2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues 18.02s and 21.01s; beat grid every 0.5s from 3.02s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (extraction helper not used; slow ambient glow drift instead)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (soft clicks on taps, bong on popups, soft impacts on scene hits)
- Restraint rule: no SFX louder than the music bed's presence.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Icon, then "Your phone is a PC dock." word by word. Audio: soft impact on landing. → crossfade
### Scene 2 — Deck — 4s (3–7)
Phone frame rises. Tap Volume up (PC volume bar fills), long-press Mute (popup), swipe to Page 3 stats. Captions: "Tap to run" / "Long-press for more" / "Swipe for pages". Sequential/interaction: yes, simulated finger. → slide
### Scene 3 — Camera — 4s (7–11)
"Your phone is also a webcam." Phone camera panel + PC viewfinder. Waiting → Live, tap 2×, tap Front. → slide
### Scene 4 — Pet — 3.5s (11–14.5)
Pet pops in and hops; chips "Plays games", "Wears outfits", "Reacts to your music" arrive one by one (hat, sunglasses appear). → crossfade
### Scene 5 — Touch and voice — 3.5s (14.5–18)
Two-finger scroll then pinch; mic pulses with "Say “hey dock”". → slide
### Scene 6 — Connect — 3s (18–21)
Real Connect screenshot lands on the 18.02s cue. "Wi-Fi or USB." → crossfade
### Scene 7 — Outro — 3s (21–24)
Icon lands on the 21.01s cue; "Free and open source."; repo address.

**Music mood:** upbeat, clean. **Audio summary:** warm bed, taps and pops tied to motion, a soft impact on the final logo.
