Phone control deck · PC companion

Your phone is a PC dock.

Turn any phone into a deck of buttons, widgets and gestures for your computer. Free and open source.

Download How to install

Windows installer Open Dock_*_x64-setup.exe · Android OpenDock.apk

What it does

Media keys

Play, skip and volume, straight from the phone.

Hotkeys

Custom keyboard shortcuts and ordered shortcut sequences.

Apps and files

Launch apps and open files with one tap.

Scripts and plugins

Run your own scripts and extend the deck with plugins.

Folders and profiles

Multiple pages, folders and profiles to organise buttons.

Widgets

Clock, now playing, lyrics and PC stats.

Voice and touchpad

Talk to your PC or use the phone as a touchpad.

Wi-Fi or USB

Pair once by QR code. Android can switch to USB.

Every phone gets its own deck.

Pair several phones to one PC and customise each one separately. Give each device a name instead of an IP address.

Install in two steps

Windows

  1. Download Open Dock_*_x64-setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Android

  1. Download OpenDock.apk from the same release.
  2. Allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
  3. Scan the QR code shown in the PC app from the phone's Connect tab.

The installer isn't signed, hence the Windows warning. After the first install, Open Dock updates itself.
